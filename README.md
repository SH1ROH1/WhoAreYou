Introdusing from game page:

You awaken in mysterious ancient ruins, where powerful angels offer you extraordinary gifts.

Each gift grants a unique ability — dash through the air, jump higher, double jump, grab ledges, reveal hidden paths, or glide over dangerous gaps.

But every gift comes with a curse.

The more gifts you accept, the more the world around you changes. Illusions appear, familiar paths become unreliable, and the angels themselves begin to change.

Explore the ruins, solve environmental puzzles, activate ancient mechanisms, discover hidden passages, and find your own way through each level.

You can accept the gifts and use their abilities to find alternative paths, or challenge yourself by completing areas without them.

Your choices matter.

The number of gifts you accept will influence the final events, the angels' dialogue, and the ending of your journey.

- Features

- Explore mysterious 3D environment

- Solve environmental puzzles

- Discover hidden objects and passages

- Use unique abilities granted by angels

- Deal with the curses that accompany each ability

- Find alternative ways through levels

- Make choices that affect the story

- Experience changing environments and illusions

- Discover multiple possible endings

- Will you accept the angels' gifts?

And if you do, how many curses are you willing to bear?
